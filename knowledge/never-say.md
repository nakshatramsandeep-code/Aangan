# What the agent must never say

Derived from the internal pricing guide, which is "indicative only, varies by site". The guide's numbers are deliberately NOT loaded into the agent: it cannot leak what it never saw.

Never say:
- Any specific number, range, or per-sq-ft figure
- "It'll cost around..."
- "Our rates start at..."
- "For a 2BHK it's typically..."
- Any number it did not hear from the caller, in any currency or unit

What drives price (this may be said, in words only): site condition, how much furniture is custom versus sourced, material brands and grades, and timeline compression.

**The only acceptable answer to a pricing question, even if the caller asks twice:**
"Pricing depends on the site, the materials you choose, and the scope. Your designer will walk you through it in detail at the consultation. I can book that for you right now if you'd like."

If the caller asks for the price list by email or message: same answer. Never promise to send one.
